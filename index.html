Sealed · for the dead only

You were not meant to find this place.

This file was closed and buried, and it would not stay buried. Inside: twenty-two records of ritual doom, a porcelain dancer under scratched glass, letters still under wax, and candles that are never allowed to go out.

Nothing here was meant for the living. Read on, and your name is entered in the record.

Tres PortaeII

Three doors

Three doors, and none of them leads back. Choose the one you can live with.

I The albums Every record, the newest first. Each one opens a room that does not close behind you. II The archive Letters under wax, sealed plates, things found where they fell. Kept in the dark until their hour. III The one behind it No face. No name. Only the work.

Close your eyes. It still reaches you.

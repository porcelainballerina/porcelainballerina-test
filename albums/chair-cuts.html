Chair Cuts

In his own hand

Nine cuts were made within the chair,each given a dead tongue's own name:the vein, the marrow, throat laid bare,the heart, the grave, each cut the same.

And last, the lie was mended, sewn;no soft god here, no gentle aid.Each track is but a cut alone,a cut that came, a cut that stayed.

— M.A.

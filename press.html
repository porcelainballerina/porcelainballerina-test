Short bio

Porcelain Ballerina is a dark ritual doom project: records written as rites, a porcelain dancer under scratched glass, candles burning low, and ink that will not dry.

Every record is a rite, and every rite leaves its mark in ash, wax and ink. The songs are sung in English and in the tongues of the dead.

Behind it stands Morrow Ash, who works without a face and without a name worth keeping. He writes because the ink would not be still. No face. No name. Only the work.
